Field notes
Q

How do I unlock Pro Mode?

Tap any Pro feature — Stats, Compare and Merge, Coverage, or Traits — or the lock icon next to them. That opens the unlock screen, where a single one-time purchase unlocks everything permanently. No subscription, and it carries over if you reinstall (use Restore Purchase on the same Apple ID).

Q

Is my data private?

Yes, by default everything you track — matchups, notes, stats, trait ratings — stays only on your device. There's a separate, off-by-default toggle in Settings to share anonymized match results to help improve the app; it's opt-in, and it never shares raw match history or anything that identifies you.

Q

Do I need an account?

No. There's no login or sign-up anywhere in the app — everything works immediately after install.

Q

I found a bug. What should I include?

Email the address above with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps a lot if it's visual. Which game (Ultimate, Melee, etc.) is also useful context.
